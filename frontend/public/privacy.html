Privacy Policy for OmniArch AI

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

OmniArch AI ("we", "our") is a free, open, and developer-first architecture visualization tool. We respect your privacy and enforce a strict Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) and Zero-Storage policy.

2. Data Processing & Ephemeral Handling

  • Code Ingestion: Your codebase files, manifests, and routes are analyzed entirely in-memory for the sole purpose of extracting architectural graphs. We do not store, index, or retain your source code on our servers.
  • Credentials & API Keys: Any custom API keys (Miro Access Tokens, ModelScope / OpenAI / Custom LLM keys) provided in the application settings are stored locally in your browser's localStorage or passed directly in ephemeral request headers. They are never logged or stored in external databases.
  • LLM Inference: Inferences are executed directly against your selected provider endpoint (e.g., Alibaba ModelScope, local Ollama, or your custom OpenAI-compatible endpoint).

3. Third-Party Integrations

When you sync architecture diagrams to Miro, OmniArch AI communicates directly with the official Miro REST API v2 using your authorized credentials to create shapes, frames, and connector elements on your designated board.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our marketplace capabilities evolve. Continued use of the application indicates acceptance of any revisions.

5. Contact & Support

For questions or support, visit our open repository on GitHub: OmniArch AI Repository.
